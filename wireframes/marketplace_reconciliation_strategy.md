# Chorus Marketplace & Premium Reconciliation Plan

## 1. The Pricing Model
To reconcile the $7.99/mo Premium plan with independent teacher rates, we propose a "Credits & Access" model:

*   **Free Users:** 280-character limit, limited AI grammar checks, pay full teacher rate for all sessions.
*   **Premium Users ($7.99/mo):** 1000-character limit, unlimited AI grammar deep-dives, and **Platform Benefits** for the marketplace:
    *   **Reduced Platform Fee:** The platform takes 10% instead of 15% from teachers when a Premium user books them, allowing teachers to offer "Premium-only" lower rates.
    *   **Monthly Credits:** Premium includes 1 "Trial Credit" per month to try a new teacher for 20 mins.
    *   **Priority Support:** Faster response times for learning-related queries.

## 2. Teacher Rates
Teachers **should** charge their own rates. This creates a healthy marketplace where experienced teachers can command a premium, while new teachers can offer lower rates to build their reputation. Chorus remains the platform and "pedagogical engine."

## 3. Teacher-Student Chat Interface
The chat between a teacher and student shouldn't just be a bubble-stream. It needs:
*   **Lesson Context:** A sticky header showing the next scheduled class or current lesson goal.
*   **Shared Sandbox:** A way for teachers to "push" vocabulary cards or grammar drills directly into the student's SRS queue.
*   **Quick Actions:** One-tap scheduling, file sharing (PDFs/HW), and voice notes.
