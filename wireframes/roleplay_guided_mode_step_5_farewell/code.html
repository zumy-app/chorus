tips_and_updates Cultural Note: Tipping in Spain

In Spain, tipping is modest (leaving small 20–50 cent coins in the counter jar el bote or rounding up). Leaving huge percentages is not required. Saying «¡Hasta luego!» or «¡Buen día!» with a smile is warmly expected!
